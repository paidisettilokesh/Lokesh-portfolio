ResuLens — AI Resume Analyzer

An AI-assisted resume review tool that evaluates ATS compatibility and offers practical improvement suggestions. I built PDF/DOCX parsing, keyword-matching logic, and an ATS compatibility score.

The responsive React and Tailwind CSS frontend connects to a Flask backend that integrates AI APIs. Tested with 20–25 sample resumes during development.

Syntra AI — Autonomous Email Intelligence

A platform that monitors Gmail inboxes and uses large language models to summarize, classify, and prioritize messages. It includes risk analysis, activity history in SQLite, and Telegram alerts for important or suspicious emails.

The system includes automated unit and integration tests, provider fallback, structured logging, and retry handling.
